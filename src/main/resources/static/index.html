Our Story

About Shree Ram Hotel

Located in the heart of Ameenpur, Miyapur, Shree Ram Hotel has been a beloved neighbourhood hotel serving the community with honest, home-style South Indian food. What began as a humble tiffin corner grew into a full-service hotel complete with our famous Curry Point — a unique counter of freshly cooked curries and dry fries prepared daily.

We serve A/C and Non-A/C dine-in, takeaway, home delivery, and we undertake all party orders and catering. Every dish is made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and masalas ground in-house — no shortcuts, just great food.
